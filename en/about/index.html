ABOUT THE PROJECT

Chess with room to play and learn.

3dXess is an independent project that brings classic chess into a three-dimensional interface, with browser-based games and training tools.

Why it exists

The idea is simple: make chess accessible, visual, and engaging without turning the game into a cluttered interface. The board is the center of the experience; menus and effects should support the game, not compete with it.

Beyond playing, you can study the rules, explore prepared positions, and solve exercises with clear goals. The project is built around three principles: clarity, responsiveness, and respect for the player's focus.

How it works

How it works

♞

3D board

Three.js renders the pieces, lighting, orbital camera, and transitions.

⌖

Rules engine

A JavaScript engine validates moves, check, castling, en passant, promotion, and draws.

↗

Online games

Online play uses WebRTC and a peer-to-peer layer to connect browsers. The host maintains the game state.

◇

Local bot

The bot search runs in the browser, with multiple levels and playing styles.

Transparency and privacy

No account is required. Online play is peer-to-peer and may expose connection information, such as an IP address, to other participants or network infrastructure. The presence counter is optional and can be disabled in settings. Exercises and daily streaks are stored in this browser's local storage; they are not a profile or global ranking.

A project in progress

The learning and practice pages are being structured to support more exercises, themed collections, and explanations. The goal is to expand the content without sacrificing performance or simplicity.
