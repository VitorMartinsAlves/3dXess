LEARNING PATH

Learn the game. See the board.

A practical path to understand the fundamentals, recognize patterns, and make better decisions — from the first move to the endgame.

Start with what matters

You do not need to memorize dozens of openings to play better. Understand the objective, master the rules, and learn to evaluate threats before going deeper into strategy.

1 · Fundamentals

Learn how each piece moves, its relative value, and the goal of checkmate.

2 · King safety

3 · Tactics

Look for undefended pieces, double attacks, pins, and mating patterns.

4 · Strategy

5 · Endgames

Understand king activity, opposition, and pawn races to promotion.
